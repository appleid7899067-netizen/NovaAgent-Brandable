# Film-Making Skill

This skill provides a structured approach to planning and executing film projects, from concept to delivery. It helps you generate production plans, scripts, storyboards, shot lists, schedules, budgets, and post-production workflows.

## When to use
- You want to plan and execute a film project (short film, documentary, music video, etc.).
- You need a clear pre-production plan (logline, synopsis, storyboard, shot list).
- You need a production plan (shooting schedule, crew roles, equipment list, location planning).
- You need a post-production plan (edit plan, sound design, color grading, final deliverables).
- You want templates and prompts to guide filmmaking tasks.

## How to use
- Provide a brief about your film (length, genre, tone, target audience, location constraints, deadlines).
- Ask for specific outputs: script outline, storyboard outline, shot list, production schedule, budget outline, or post-production plan.
- The skill will generate reusable resources and a structured workflow in response to your prompts.

## Outputs
- Production plan (pre-production, production, post-production)
- Screenplay outline or logline (as requested)
- Storyboard outline (scene-by-scene)
- Shot list (with locations, time estimates, and camera/angle notes)
- Shooting schedule (timeline with day-by-day plan)
- Equipment list (cameras, lenses, sound, lighting)
- Budget outline (ballpark figures, costs, contingencies)
- Post-production plan (edits, sound, color, visual effects)

## Example prompts
- Create a production plan for a 4-minute sci-fi short dealing with time travel. Output should include logline, synopsis, storyboard outline, shot list, production schedule, and rough budget.
- Plan a documentary about a local artist: outline the structure, interview questions, location plan, and post-production workflow.
- Script and storyboard request: "Write a 2-page script for a romance short and outline a 10-scene storyboard with shot descriptions."

## Best practices
- Start with a concise concept before expanding into details.
- Provide constraints (length, schedule, budget) to tailor outputs.
- Keep outputs modular so they can be reused in future projects.
- Use imperative language in SKILL body to guide the agent.
