---
title: AI Office - Lean Business Canvas
---

Problem
- Many small businesses lack scalable automation strategies and affordable AI-assisted workflow automation.
- Current tools are fragmented and require heavy integration.

Solution
- A turnkey AI Office that designs, builds, and maintains automated workflows and CLI-based tools for clients.
- Provides pre-built, reusable CLI harnesses and agent-ready SKILLs to accelerate ROI.

Unique Value Proposition
- One-stop AI automation studio with plug-and-play CLI harnesses and an end-to-end harness pipeline, enabling non-experts to automate complex tasks quickly.

Customer Segments
- Small to medium businesses seeking automation.
- Freelancers/consultants needing repeatable automation patterns.
- Teams adopting AI agents to orchestrate internal tools.

Channels
- Direct sales, online marketing, partnerships with MSPs, and onboarding via CLI-Hub style discovery.

Revenue Streams
- Monthly retainer for managed automation services.
- Project-based automation buildouts.
- Licensing for reusable SKILLs and CLI harnesses.
- Training and support subscriptions.

Cost Structure
- Staffing for AI/automation engineers, product, and sales.
- Cloud compute costs for running automations and agents.
- Tooling/licenses and security/compliance.

Key Metrics
- Number of automations deployed, time-to-value, monthly recurring revenue, client retention, NPS.

Unfair Advantage
- Existing CLi harness library (CLI-Anything) and a pipeline for generating new CLIs and SKILLs quickly.
